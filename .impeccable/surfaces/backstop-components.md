# Surface brief: Backstop app (all three views)

Mode: Operate. Replacement of the neumorphic/soft-UI world. Product truth in PRODUCT.md.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every screen is an easy-read page: one short sentence, one drawn picture, one button. It refuses the category-default soft card on a tinted ground with a blue pill button.

OWN-WORLD: Deep navy ink (#0B2A6F family) on white, banded with pale blue; a marker yellow used only as a highlighter behind the one key phrase and on the brand shield. State colours are signal green, orange, red, each with an icon and a word. Type is Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, heavy for headings. Everything has a 3px navy outline and square-ish 12px corners. No shadows, no gradients. Pictures are bold outline drawings in a 3px stroke, set in square picture tiles.

STORY: A worried reader understands in seconds what is happening to their mail and presses the one obvious button. The helper sees the verdict and acts with confidence.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Navy header band with the yellow shield and wordmark. Below, a huge marker-underlined title, then picture tile plus one status sentence, then one big primary button, then message rows each led by a picture tile. The primary action is full width at the foot of the first block.

FORM: Easy Read Page, IMPECCABLE'S PICK, position 1 of 7. seed:f7ed0271acdb51c8

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
